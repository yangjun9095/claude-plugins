# Principles

This file compares the checked profiles of Hernan G. Garcia, Loic A. Royer, Jay Shendure, Cole Trapnell, Luca Pinello, James Zou and Alexander Rives. A move is a closed answer to a checklist item. A count reads N of M papers: N papers make the move, out of M papers that the item covers. The counts include first-author papers and essays. Trainees draft most senior-author papers, so each count describes the papers of a group. The checklist could not measure excitement reliably in any profile, so this file states no pattern about excitement. The essay item enters neither section, because fewer than 5 people have an essay in their sample.

## Shared moves

A move is shared when at least 5 people make it in at least M divided by 2 of their papers. For introduction paragraphs and results sections, a paper counts when any one of them makes the move.
